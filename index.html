Our Story

More Than Just BBQ,
It's Family.

Since 2010, Heards BBQ & Soul Food has been serving up the most authentic, mouth-watering BBQ and soul food this side of the Mississippi. What started as a family dream has grown into a beloved community gathering place.

Our recipes have been passed down through generations, each dish crafted with love, patience, and the finest ingredients. From our slow-smoked ribs that fall right off the bone to our creamy mac and cheese that tastes just like grandma made it – every bite tells a story.

We believe in the power of good food to bring people together. Whether you're celebrating a milestone, grabbing lunch with coworkers, or feeding the whole family, we're honored to be part of your table.
